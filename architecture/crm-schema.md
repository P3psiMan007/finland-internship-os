# CRM Schema

Live source of truth: Google Sheet `Finland Internship CRM — Clean v1`.

## PROSPECTS
Important fields include company/domain/city, lane, target function, role URL, English evidence, compensation evidence, business trigger, score, status, contact provenance, application status, send/reply lifecycle, next action, notes and Gmail IDs.

## ACTIVITY
Append-only lifecycle/event evidence for discovery, qualification, sends, replies, applications and learning events.

## EXPERIMENTS
Tracks one or two active targeting/copy experiments with minimum sample thresholds and outcome decisions.

## CONTROL
Campaign-level settings. Current worker prompts sometimes contain policy directly instead of consistently reading CONTROL, creating drift risk.
